ACME Cloud Billing

Plans. ACME Cloud has three plans: Starter at 19 dollars per month, Team at 49
dollars per month, and Business at 199 dollars per month. All plans are billed
monthly in advance and can be cancelled at any time from the billing dashboard.

Refunds. New customers can request a full refund within 14 days of their first
payment. After 14 days, charges are non-refundable, but you can downgrade or
cancel to avoid the next charge.

Invoices. Invoices are generated on the first day of each billing cycle and are
available as PDF downloads under Settings, Billing, Invoices. Each invoice shows
the plan, the billing period, taxes, and the payment method used.

Failed payments. If a payment fails, ACME retries the charge after 3 days and
again after 7 days. If it still fails, the account moves to a read-only state
until the balance is paid.
